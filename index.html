“
Jasmine Bharucha is an author, mother of two and an internationally renowned singer/songwriter who was the first performer from India to appear on MTV Asia in 1990 as a young teenager. She has been featured in Rolling Stone Magazine, Time Magazine and other international media, and has been nominated as the Best Female Pop Artist at the Channel V Music Awards in Asia.

The song Fog Horn Blowing is a tribute to Steveston B.C. Jasmine moved here in 1996 and fell madly in love with the quaint fishing village leaving behind the limelight of the music industry. Jasmine explains her intention and inspiration in writing the song:

“I wanted the listener to feel relaxed and calm just as I do sitting by the water each evening. The Sky and water were all I needed to bring me an inner stillness and joy that no lofty ambition could ever bring. I want my music to bring a moment of happiness to the listener so they too can forget their troubles,roll their shoulders back and just enjoy the moment."

Jasmine Bharucha
